### FFmpeg in the installer · FFmpeg dans l'installeur

The installer (not the portable build) ships an **unmodified LGPL build** of FFmpeg from
[BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds](https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds) (`ffmpeg-n8.1.3-18-g46d899d7a0-win64-lgpl-shared`,
release `autobuild-2026-10-18-13-01`). It contains no GPL or non-free component. It is a separate executable plus its
DLLs, in the `ffmpeg` folder next to the app, started as an external process: you can replace those files with your
own build. Its license is in `ffmpeg\LICENSE.txt`, and `ffmpeg\SOURCE.txt` links to the exact
[source commit](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/46d899d7a0) and to the build scripts. The build and its SHA-256
are pinned in `scripts/ffmpeg.json`; nothing is committed to this repository.
/ L'installeur (pas la version portable) embarque une **build LGPL non modifiée** de FFmpeg, issue de
BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds. Aucun composant GPL ou non libre. C'est un exécutable séparé avec ses DLL, dans le dossier
`ffmpeg` à côté de l'app, lancé comme processus externe : tu peux le remplacer par ta propre build. La licence est
dans `ffmpeg\LICENSE.txt`, et `ffmpeg\SOURCE.txt` renvoie au commit source exact et aux scripts de build.
